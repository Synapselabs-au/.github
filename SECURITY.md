# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not report suspected vulnerabilities in a public issue. Email a
clear description, affected repository or service, reproduction steps, and any
relevant proof of concept to
[jordan@synapselabs.com.au](mailto:jordan@synapselabs.com.au).

We will review the report in confidence and work to understand and address
valid security issues. Please give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate
before disclosing the issue publicly.

## Scope

This policy applies to public repositories owned by the Synapselabs-au GitHub
organisation and their first-party services. Third-party services and
dependencies should be reported to their respective maintainers.
