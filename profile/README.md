# Synapse Labs

> Building technology ventures for the long term.

Synapse Labs is a privately held Australian company that builds, operates, and
compounds technology businesses. We create useful products, stay close to the
work of operating them, and take a patient approach to ownership.

## How we build

We treat venture building as an operating discipline:

- **Build** — start with a real problem, validate demand, and focus the
  strongest product thesis.
- **Operate** — stay close to customers, systems, and economics so decisions
  reflect how the business actually works.
- **Compound** — reuse what we learn and reinvest patiently in ventures with
  durable potential.

## Long-term ownership

Our immediate priority is building and operating our own technology ventures
well. We are also interested in confidential conversations with owners and
founders of established technology businesses considering succession,
partnership, or a long-term home for their company.
