# Code of Conduct

Synapse Labs is committed to a professional, constructive, and inclusive
collaboration environment. We expect everyone participating in our public
repositories to:

- communicate respectfully and in good faith;
- focus feedback on ideas and work, not individuals;
- respect differing experiences and perspectives; and
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.

If you experience or witness conduct that does not meet these expectations,
please report it privately to
[jordan@synapselabs.com.au](mailto:jordan@synapselabs.com.au). Reports will be
reviewed in confidence and appropriate action will be taken.
